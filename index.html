Core Product

Active Cabin Comfort Module (ACCM)

Product Objective

Designed for Control and Functional Stability

Our ACCM is designed for cabin environment control and functional stability, with a focus on:

  • Control accuracy
  • System stability
  • Predictable performance

across operating conditions. The module aims to achieve a reliable balance among competing performance requirements, while supporting robustness and consistency in mass production.

Design Decision Approach

Artificial Intelligence (AI) at the Core of Decision Making

We have structured our NPD process by placing AI at the core of decision making. Deep-learning models analyze comprehensive datasets to generate optimized solutions, which directly guide downstream development activities. Our engineering team focuses on process oversight and compliance verification, rather than detailed parameter optimization.
